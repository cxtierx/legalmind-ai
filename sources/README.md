# Legal source set

## Included scope

- Part III, Fundamental Rights, Articles 12–28.
- Article 19 is the first longer provision selected for question-answering practice.
- No other Parts or Articles are in this source set. Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII, Articles 352–360) are excluded.

## Official source

- **Title:** The Constitution of India (Fourth English-Kannada diglot edition)
- **Source:** Government of India, Ministry of Law and Justice, Legislative Department
- **Text date:** As on 11 November 2025
- **Official PDF:** https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/359f70a69695affb9d72f8393102bd2e.pdf
- **Printed pages used:** 6–15 for Part III, Articles 12–28
- **Local source text:** `Part_III_Fundamental_Rights_Articles_12-28.txt`

The local text is an excerpt of the English text for the selected Articles. Clause order in Article 19(1) has been normalized for readability; the official PDF remains the authoritative reference.

## Use in LegalMind

The app can look up an Article number or search for matching words locally. Articles 14, 19, and 21 also show short sample summaries. No AI service is connected, and the summaries are still written into the app rather than generated from retrieved text. Topic search is exact word matching, not semantic understanding.

Keep answers limited to the included Articles, cite the Article and clause used, and say when the source text does not answer a question. Do not present a summary as legal advice or as an explanation of court decisions. Before any public release, check for amendments or official updates after 11 November 2025.
